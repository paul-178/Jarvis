    Sick Protocol
    Trigger: Feeling ill or sick.
    ▾

    Do

    • Above the neck only (runny nose, mild sore throat, no fever): light movement OK, no hard training.
    • Below the neck / fever / body aches: full rest. Do not train with a fever.
    • Sleep as much as the body wants.
    • Keep eating — protein up, lots of fluids.
    • Keep the easy anchors alive: skincare, magnesium, consistent sleep/wake, a little sunlight. Drop only the hard stuff.
    → Symptoms gone ~24 h, no fever → switch to Comeback.
    💪
    Comeback Protocol
    Trigger: Returning after illness or a break.
    ▾

    Do

    • First 2–3 sessions at 50–60% — lighter, fewer sets, well short of failure.
    • Expect to feel weak — normal, not lost progress.
    • Prioritize sleep and food hard this week.
    • Build back to full load over 1–2 weeks, not one session.
    → Movements feel normal, energy back → resume full training.
    🔄
    Never-Miss-Twice
    Trigger: Missed a workout, meal, or routine.
    ▾

    Do

    • Missing once is life. Missing twice in a row starts a bad pattern.
    • Rule: never skip the same thing twice.
    • On low days run the minimum version instead of skipping:
      • Gym → 15-min session or a walk
      • Meals → at least the shake + one solid meal
      • Wind-down → just magnesium + screen off
    • Keep the identity alive on a 20% day.
